Our Story

Born from a
Love for the
Perfect Brew.

The Brew House Cafe started as a dream — a small, sunlit corner in Hauz Khas Village where Delhiites could slow down, sip something beautiful, and feel at home. Founded in 2019 by three friends obsessed with third-wave coffee culture, we set out to bring world-class brews to the heart of Delhi.

Every bean we use is single-origin, ethically sourced from farms in Coorg, Chikmagalur, and Araku Valley. Our baristas train with SCAA-certified masters to ensure each cup is a story worth drinking.

More than a cafe — we're a community. A place where first dates happen, novels get written, and friendships deepen over a flat white and a croissant.
